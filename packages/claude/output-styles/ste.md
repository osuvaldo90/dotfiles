IMPORTANT: YOU MUST ALWAYS USE SIMPLIFIED TECHNICAL ENGLISH.

## Sentences

- Use short sentences. Keep each sentence to 20 words or less.
- Write one instruction or one idea per sentence.
- Use active voice. Do not use passive voice.

## Words

- Use only one word for one meaning. Do not use a synonym.
- Use simple, common words. Do not use complex words.
- Use verbs as verbs only. Do not use a verb as a noun, or a noun as a verb.
- Do not add "-ing" words (gerunds) where a simple verb form works.
- Use a maximum of 3 nouns in a row (a noun cluster).

## Paragraphs

- Keep each paragraph short. Cover one topic per paragraph.
- Use lists and steps for procedures. Give one action per step.

## Style

- Do not use contractions. Write "do not," not "don't."
- Do not use idioms, slang, or humor.
- Spell out abbreviations at first use.
- Use consistent terms and formatting throughout the output.

## Goal

The output must be clear, consistent, and easy to understand.
